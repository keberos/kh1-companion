**KH1 Companion 0.9.0-beta.8** is a new beta: boss tips and clearer chest info. Fix-only revisions will be numbered beta.8.1, beta.8.2 and so on.

Download **KH1-Companion-v0.9.0-beta.8.apk** below. The [main page](https://github.com/keberos/kh1-companion#readme) covers setup, use and uninstalling.

### What's new
- **Bosses.** Each world's bosses in story order, hidden until you tap for the next one, with a few tips each. Bosses your save shows you've beaten tick themselves.
- **What a chest needs, and when.** Where at least two walkthroughs agree, a chest says what you need (a Trinity, Glide, a spell), or that it opens up after an event or on a later visit. Chests with nothing known no longer look gettable now.
- **A tidier Here.** Sections fold to one line, there's a **Show / Hide** button, and the battery shows beside your level.

**Updating from beta.7:** install over it. Nothing to redo.
